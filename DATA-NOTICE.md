# ONC-AGI world data: notice

These worlds are derived from real, de-identified human cohort data. Each world resamples
real records, adds small observation noise, replaces feature names and patient identifiers,
and generates the outcome synthetically. Measured values remain close to the source records.
The source of each world is the `source` field of `manifests/*.json`.

Conditions of use (all sources):
- Do not attempt to identify, contact or learn the identity of any person whose data
  contributed to these worlds, and do not link these data with individually identifiable
  data from any other source.
- Worlds derived from NHANES (`source: nhanes`) are for statistical reporting and analysis
  only, and may not be used for research on re-identification or disclosure-control methods
  (NCHS Data User Agreement, https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/policy/data-user-agreement.html).
- Credit the sources when you publish results that use these worlds.
