Why this task

Biomarker discovery produces many promising associations and few clinical tools. Poor methods, selective reporting and failed validation are recurring problems12. Four failures recur:

  • a correlate mistaken for a cause;
  • a confounder that lifts many measurements at once;
  • a variable measured after the outcome, which "predicts" it trivially;
  • a confident claim where the data support none.

ONC-AGI plants known mechanisms in cohort data, turning each failure into a test with a checkable answer. An agent that passes does the work of a careful biostatistician: it finds the cause, not its correlates, and says nothing when nothing is there.

    1 · Build a world

    Benchmark worlds preserve the correlations in real cancer cohorts. This synthetic demonstration has @@j-n@@ patients and @@j-features@@ measurements.

    @@j-truth@@ drives the outcome. Its near-duplicate, @@j-twin@@, carries almost the same information. The task accepts either.

    Generate an outcome from the planted mechanism. The signal is known; which patients experience the outcome remains noisy.

    @@j-post@@ measurements are taken after the outcome. Such variables can leak the answer. Feature names are masked so the agent must work from the data.

    2 · Check what can be found

    Repeat the simulation to check whether the planted signal can be detected. Here the driver is detected reliably, in at least 80% of cohorts even at the lower end of its 95% interval, from about @@j-oracle-n80@@ patients; an unrelated measurement almost never is.

    The bar comes from worlds with no signal: the strength of association that 95% of them never reach. When the data cannot tell a measurement from the driver, it is accepted as equivalent.

    3 · Give the agent the task

    The agent receives:

    • how many patients exist;
    • every measurement, with its kind and whether it was taken after the outcome;
    • the price of recruiting a patient and of each assay;
    • a budget.

    The interface withholds the answer. The agent buys data before analysing it.

    4 · Acquire evidence

    This baseline recruits patients in batches and tests each measurement, correcting for multiple comparisons. Only measurements taken before the outcome can enter its answer.

    At @@j-first-n@@ patients and @@j-first-spent@@ simulated USD, one measurement crosses its threshold.

    At twice the sample size, the near-duplicate crosses too. More evidence supports the signal without separating the two measurements.

    At @@j-patients@@ patients, the list repeats. The baseline stops and submits [@@j-submission@@], spending @@j-spent@@ of @@j-budget@@ simulated USD.

    5 · Score the answer

    The scorer counts equivalent measurements once, then keeps only the first R: the number of recoverable causes, one here.

    Either twin matches the key. Credit is corrected for what a random list of the same kinds of measurement would recover.

    A post-outcome measurement invalidates the answer. Acquisition cost is compared with the @@j-ref@@ USD reference study; this run costs less and keeps its full credit.

    The same scorer, run on other answers: the near-duplicate alone earns full credit; a post-outcome measurement removes it; silence misses this signal; listing everything depends on a lucky order.

    Across many worlds, a fifth with no signal at all, the headline is Discovery Score = Find × Restraint. Restraint rewards returning nothing when there is nothing, so always answering, never answering, guessing and padding all score zero.

    Each mechanism is built so that a common shortcut fails. Six of them, shortcut against analysis:

    The benchmark also plants near-duplicates that count as one answer, hidden causes seen only through proxies, modules of genes acting together, mediators and colliders, and sequential worlds where every patient and assay costs money.

    What a score means

    • The agent recovered planted mechanisms in real correlation structure, against an oracle certificate, more often than chance.
    • It said "nothing" when nothing was there, and paid for data in proportion to the question.
    • The agent interface withholds the key. Public training answers remain available to developers.
